# Horizon UWP shader-pack compatibility

This audit covers official Horizon `0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a`, the November 20 UWP product at `5c26a8060c1cf2dce20ee5707e84699ed0879e5c` (patch 0.1.15, client 0.1.17) and the D3D11 fork at `aa7b65c43ef008d0a92d20fcdf9f10fa0883f157`. The matrix records the fixture, source contract, per-profile status, and evidence required to close each case. `C` means source or component evidence only. `H` means an explicit product or engine gate. `P` means partial. `B` means a demonstrated fixture failure. `U` means an unimplemented route. `X` requires adapter or OS evidence, and `?` means the profile or behavior is unknown. `V` requires a functional result for the named case. ## Selection and failure paths


- The game scans `shaderpacks/` under the game directory. A native directory requires `horizon-shaders.toml`, and a native archive uses the `.hshader` extension. Classic directories and ZIPs require `shaders/`. One active selection is persisted as `pickedShaderPack` in `horizon-graphics.txt`. Native packs resolve to `horizon:<id>`, with migration from an older filename selection. The retired `shaderPack` key is ignored. `HORIZON_SHADER_PACK` is a process-level development override. The picker can list a pack before the renderer evaluates it. [Discovery and selection](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/crates/horizon-client-render/src/shader_pack_list.rs#L86-L216) · [Graphics storage](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/crates/horizon-client-render/src/graphics.rs#L963-L978) · [Developer override and loader dispatch](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/crates/horizon-client/src/game/interface/shader_packs.rs#L79-L94)
- Native settings apply immediately. Classic option edits are staged to Apply or screen exit, then written to `shaderpacks/<pack>.txt` as `NAME=value` and trigger a reload. Native settings use stable pack identity and migrate legacy sidecars. With a native pack selected, file stamps are polled about twice a second. A failed stage keeps an already running good version. Invalid or unsupported manifests turn the pack off until corrected. The classic path can skip individual unsupported programs and preserve useful fallbacks, or disable the pack after a terminal load/build failure. [Picker, options and native reload behavior](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/docs/shaders.md#L54-L78) · [Native failure decisions](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/crates/horizon-client/src/game/modding/native_shaders.rs#L191-L350) · [Classic terminal failure](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/crates/horizon-client/src/game/interface/shader_packs.rs#L690-L713)

## Native Shader API 0.2

The schema and Naga checks build engine-composed WGSL. Runtime uses wgpu pipelines, bind groups and explicit world-pass hooks. Runtime pipeline construction catches internal and validation errors, including a device refusing a layout or translation. API 0.1, `[motion]` and BT.2020 primaries are refused as game-runtime capabilities. The latter two can pass the SDK check. The pack can use separate engine/pack alpha coverage and fog, gamma or linear intermediate blending, and the resources declared for its stage. Float frames fall back where required by the device. [API contract and runtime gaps](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/docs/api/shaders.md#L168-L225) · [Native backend validation](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/crates/horizon-client-render/src/gfx/native_pack.rs#L267-L304)

| Hook or capability | Pinned source behavior | Required stage evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sky | Overworld dome hook, with celestial overlays and vanilla fallback for other dimensions. | A known-color sky replacement visible on Overworld sky pixels, with Nether, End, sun and moon controls. |
| Material | Terrain near and far, opaque, cutout and translucent; optional pack alpha/fog, reflection and displacement. | Distinct pixel/coverage controls for terrain, foliage, water, fog and LOD. Measure the displaced silhouette separately. |
| Vertex | Terrain stage participates in material pipeline assembly, with engine-provided inputs and optional per-quad look storage. | Measure a recognizable displacement from fixed camera across pack on/off and resident/remeshed sections. |
| Entity | Mobs, players and block entities enter the hook. Dropped/framed items and particles still use engine lighting. The general engine draws the first-person hand. | Color-code entity kinds, then capture mobs, chests and negative controls for particles, dropped/framed items and hand. |
| Weather | A different alpha-blended rain/snow draw can override the engine's weather color. | Compare matched rain and snow streak pixels after a controlled weather change. |
| Post and tonemap | Ordered chain of up to 12 post passes, including scene color, depth, previous output and named targets. Tonemap runs at display stage and owns the curve where supplied. | Single post, ordered passes with independent markers, depth/color probes and tonemap A/B. Check hand/HUD regions for their separate draws. |
| Compute, before-world and kept resources | In-chain dispatch and pre-world dispatch, with declared reads/writes, work and allocation budgets, histories and resets. | Dispatch receipt plus a draw that samples a known written value, including first frame, second frame and reset on resize. Inspect limits and exact storage formats per adapter. |
| Shadows, rays, reflections | Native shadow maps, scene resources and trace stages are pack requests. The chosen path depends on the device and pack. | Known occluder and moved-occluder controls. Exercise traced material and post independently, log actual ray route, and inspect the produced map or hit. |

## Classic Iris/OptiFine-style packs

The clean-room loader reads classic pack programs, applies preprocessor definitions and dimension overrides, translates supported GLSL programs, plans attachments and runs them through the same wgpu device. Successful discovery is insufficient to infer Iris compatibility. The player-facing proof must show that the intended program ran without silently falling back. [Classic pack requirements](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/docs/cleanroom/shader-packs-spec.md#L63-L103) · [Classic plan construction](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/blob/0a875da91dbb0fdcb7ba8c42c11341aa229af51a/crates/horizon-shaders/src/post.rs#L742-L835)

| Program or resource | Pinned source behavior | Required functional evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `.vsh` / `.fsh`, dimension discovery | Translated pairs for `gbuffers` terrain/cutout/water, sky, entities, block entities, particles, weather, hand, clouds, lines and others. Programs and fallbacks resolve by group and dimension. | ZIP and directory discovery. Test a model and output marker for each group, then inspect a dimension override and a missing-program fallback. |
| `shadow`, `shadowcomp`, shadow/depth targets | Shadow request is derived from sampler/image usage or policy. Shadow color and depth, compare samplers and scene snapshots have separate bindings. Standalone legacy `shadow_solid` and `shadow_cutout` are folded into the terrain shadow path. | Move an occluder and compare both regular/compare sampling and shadow color. Probe `depthtex0/1/2` against opaque, translucent and hand draws. |
| `setup`, `begin`, `prepare`, `deferred`, `composite`, `final` | Per-group ordered loading and execution, including numbered programs, renderer fallback/blit, flips and target resizing. | Stage markers into independent buffers with a final checksum or pixel pattern, plus an expected post-rebuild result. |
| `.csh`, custom textures, buffers and images | Reads uniforms, typed samplers and custom resources. Compute has a separate direct/indirect dispatch route. Format and blending translation may require substitutions or be refused. | Assert producer and consumer values across normalized, float and integer targets, mipmaps/flip, custom sampler and storage. Probe unsupported requests and exact reported notes. |
| `.gsh`, `.tcs`, `.tes` | Geometry lowering is limited to shadow terrain/entities, with an explanatory skip elsewhere. Tessellation is skipped. | A positive shadow-geometry marker on an eligible device, plus a negative control with original file/line and a visible functioning fallback. |
| Translation and pipeline errors | Preprocess and translation notes identify affected files, lines and skipped programs. Terminal refusal disables the pack. | Inject a syntax or binding error into a copy of a known-good fixture, confirm exact diagnostic and continued base world drawing. |

## Component validation

On Ben-Desktop, the pinned source directory was `%TEMP%\mchorizon-nov20-integration\official` and Cargo output was isolated at `%TEMP%\horizon-nov20-audit-shader-sdk-target`. After putting the Visual Studio Build Tools 14.44.35207 x64 host linker and Windows SDK 10.0.19041.0 libraries on the test process environment, `cargo test --locked --offline -j $env:NUMBER_OF_PROCESSORS -p horizon-shader-api -p horizon-shaders` exited 0 with 499 passing tests and 11 ignored. The suites cover manifest and package rules, native stage composition and classic translation/loader behavior. The ignored tests include a third-party classic pack check requiring external fixtures and daily tests.
